Meaning of each part of kalman.filter.py file
self.mean_ = estimated angular velocity
self.variance_ = uncertainty of that estimate
self.motion_ = change in angular velocity from odom
self.imu_angular_z_ = new IMU angular velocity reading
self.motion_variance_ = uncertainty in the motion model
self.measurement_variance_ = uncertainty in the IMU measurement

The important takeaway
Mental model should be:

prediction step = prior
measurement update = posterior
posterior is the final best estimate for this time step
posterior becomes the prior for the next time step
